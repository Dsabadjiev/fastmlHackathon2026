# FastML Hackathon 2026 at UCSD

Challenge 1: Di-Higgs (HH→4b vs. QCD) discrimination for trigger systems

*Task:* Binary classification of events as either HH→4b signal or QCD (or other) background.\
*Motivation:* Identifying boosted Higgs bosons decaying to bottom-quark pairs in the presence of QCD multi-jet background is one of the most important tasks at the HL-LHC and serves as the canonical benchmark for event-tagging algorithms. In particular at the trigger stage, an efficient identification of HH(4b) events is key to recording as many signal events as possible.\
*Dataset:* 10 million each HH→4b signal and QCD_HT250toInf background events. 5 million additional background events (TT, W+Jets) (trigger level objects).\
*Evaluation:* Area under the ROC curve (AUC) for the network score.
